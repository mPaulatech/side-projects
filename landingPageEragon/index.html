THE AUTHOR BEHIND THE WORLD

Christopher Paolini

Raised in Montana, Christopher Paolini began writing Eragon at fifteen. What
started as a self-published adventure became the internationally bestselling Inheritance
Cycle. His love of wild landscapes, intricate languages, and epic storytelling brings
Alagaësia to life. He also explores distant worlds through the science-fiction novels of
the Fractalverse.
