Together with Saphira, he must discover what it means to be a Dragon Rider—and decide the fate of Alagaësia.

THE AUTHOR BEHIND THE WORLD

Christopher Paolini

Raised in Montana, Christopher Paolini began writing Eragon at fifteen. What started as a self-published adventure became the internationally bestselling Inheritance Cycle. His love of wild landscapes, intricate languages, and epic storytelling brings Alagaësia to life. He also explores distant worlds through the science-fiction novels of the Fractalverse.
